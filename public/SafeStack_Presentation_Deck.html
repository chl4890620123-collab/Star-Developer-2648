The Real-World Problem

Why High-Rise Construction Yards Fail in Gusts

Elevation Wind Amplification ($z/z_0$)

Winds of 10 m/s at ground level routinely amplify to over 16–22 m/s at the 12th floor (45m) due to reduced ground friction and building corner vortices. Ground safety managers cannot visually gauge this critical elevation threshold.

The Guardrail Overtopping Trap

Insulation or formwork stacked to 2.2m behind a standard 1.2m perimeter guardrail creates a dangerous pivot point. When high winds strike, cargo leverages directly over the railing and plunges into street-level areas.

Delayed Action Window

When sudden squalls strike, site personnel cannot manually inspect 20+ scattered yard locations to check which bundles lack ratchet straps or friction pads.

Lack of Verifiable Audit Logs

Regulatory compliance requires real-time quantitative audit trails of structural fastening and safety factors, yet field teams still depend on static paper checklists.

Engineering Formulation

The 5 Aerodynamic & Mechanical Failure Modes

1. Sliding (미끄러짐)
FS = (μ·(Mg + F_strap - F_lift)) / (0.5·ρ·V²·Cd·A)

Friction resistance vs. aerodynamic drag force. Rubber pads raise μ from 0.35 to 0.65.

2. Toppling (전도)
FS = (W_base/2 · Restoring) / (H/2 · Drag)

Overturning moment at center of pressure vs. restoring moment from dunnage width and straps.

3. Uplift (들림)
FS = (Mg + F_strap) / (0.5·ρ·V²·Cl·A_top)

Evaluates aerodynamic Bernoulli lift forces on flat panel packages and sheet cargo.

4. Perimeter Fall (추락/낙하)
FS_fall = (d_edge/2.0) · min(FS_s, FS_t) · f(H_rail, H_stack)

Dual-elevation rule: penalizes stack heights exceeding guardrail height (+ΔH).

5. Scattering (비산)
V_crit = √((2·σ·g) / (ρ·Cd))

Surface density (σ = M/A). Loose EPS insulation flies at 8.8 m/s unless tightly strapped.

Standard Safety Verdicts
DANGER: FS < 1.0 (Immediate Stop)
WARNING: 1.0 ≤ FS < 1.3 (Tie-down)
CHECK: FS ≥ 1.3 (Routine Patrol)
